✨ About TravelMate

TravelMate is your AI-powered travel assistant built to simplify trip planning. It curates destinations, itineraries, and recommendations that match your interests, budget, and trip duration — all in seconds.

🌍 What It Does

  • 🧠 Personalized AI-based destination and itinerary suggestions
  • 📅 Instant day-by-day travel plans
  • 🌦️ Real-time weather integration
  • 📸 Visual insights from Unsplash

⚙️ How It Works

  1. 📝 You enter trip preferences (type, budget, interests, duration)
  2. 🤖 AI processes data and suggests smart travel plans
  3. 🗓️ You get a downloadable, detailed itinerary instantly

🔮 Future Plans

  • 📱 Mobile app for better accessibility
  • 🧭 Advanced AI model for hyper-personalized travel
  • 👥 Community trip-sharing features
